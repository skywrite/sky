# Q1 Pricing Strategy

## Context
Need to decide on pricing adjustments for Q1 product releases.

## Options Considered
- Maintain current pricing
- 10% increase across the board
- Tiered pricing based on features

## Rationale
Tiered pricing aligns with our value-based positioning.

## Decision
We will implement tiered pricing based on features, with three tiers: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise.

## Outcome
Implemented tiered pricing starting February 1st. Initial customer feedback positive.
