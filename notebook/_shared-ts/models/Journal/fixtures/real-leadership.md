## EVERY-DAY
- 0.1: Describe a recent time where you could have or did communicate "the why".
- 0.1: Describe a recent time where you could have or did set clear expectations.
- 0.1: Have you ensured your direct reports have all the resources they need to be successful recently?
- 0.1: Does anyone on your team need feedback? If so, when can you deliver this feedback? Commit to a date/time.
- 0.2: Describe a time where you let mediocrity slip or did not let it slip? If you did, what can you take action on? Commit to a date/time.
