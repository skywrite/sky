# Trying an outcome with several partner conversations

Status: rationale for an interactive mockup, not a new persistence contract.
All entities, conversation states, dates, and messages are synthetic.

The initial examples mostly began near completion. Reviewing a finished draft or
recording a repair did not exercise uncertainty across several conversations.
The wallet partnership example starts with a commercial outcome whose viability
is still open. No partner capability, offer, rate, or legal conclusion is assumed.

Four candidates are possible paths to one accepted partnership. Giving each
conversation a separate workstream would make the owner reconstruct the shared
outcome. Compact conversation rows therefore sit inside the existing detail
page, under its next action and Sky’s current contribution. The rest of the plan,
finish criteria, and history remain collapsed.

The mock separates administrative progress from commercial success. Review of
an NDA, each party’s signature, a drafted message, a sent message, and a partner’s
reply are distinct recorded events. A positive reply still needs team validation.
Accepting terms and recording a signed partnership agreement require explicit
human confirmation; an NDA never substitutes for that agreement.

Sky’s useful role here is maintaining follow-through across the conversations.
The prototype demonstrates drafts for review and explicitly simulated sends.
Recording an incoming update can retire an obsolete draft; reopening an existing
draft must not create another. A person still reviews and signs documents and
accepts commercial terms. This does not extend the local execution pilot’s
authority to external messages or signatures.

The new example is added to saved demo state without resetting previous edits.
Conversation fields remain mockup data until a later implementation establishes
their owning records and integration with existing people, projects, decisions,
and source artifacts. The current design remains [README.md](README.md).
