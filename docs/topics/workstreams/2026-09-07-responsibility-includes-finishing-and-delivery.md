# Responsibility includes finishing and delivery

The first main-app loop could prepare a useful artifact and revisit it later,
but every action still ended at owner review. Decisions never advanced without
the owner, and a reporting cadence produced a document rather than delivering an
update. That did not fulfill the intended continuing responsibility.

The next execution contracts let the owner delegate an actual result. A decision
has specific permitted choices, scope, source requirements, assumptions, and
stakeholder input. The model assesses the choice; a deterministic controller
enforces authority and retains the evidence behind the recorded resolution.
Confirmed assumptions carry source versions. A local deliverable can likewise
finish its action when an independent assessment supports the owner's success
criteria with evidence from the actual artifact. Decisions and deliverables
unblock subsequent actions without another manual prompt.

Recurring reporting has its own exact destination and audience grant. Gmail and
Slack transports return provider receipts. The delivery intent is saved before
the send, and uncertain effects are reconciled before another attempt. A report
that was committed before an interruption can resume its original delivery
without regeneration. Source changes, revoked authority, missing inputs, and
missing presentation or recording links remain reviewable obstacles. The record
distinguishes preparation, review, sending, confirmed delivery, and uncertainty.

Authority remains outside notebook prose. Both the work revision and current
permission are checked at the effect boundary. Changing a title cannot quietly
reuse the previous decision or deliverable scope. Ordinary cadence adjustments
do not renew stale activity authority. An optional lifetime limit supports work
that continues indefinitely under its daily effort limit.

Verification uses the real store, routes, model boundaries, recurring runner,
and delivery controller with synthetic transport receipts. A live model trial
also exposed a provider grammar-size limit that scripted responses could not
detect. Complex review responses use JSON generation followed by the same strict
local schema validation before effects; independent deliverable assessments use
their smaller schema. No invalid model response gains authority by fallback.
