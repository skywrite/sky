## Usage Notes

- **Sector precedence** (for tie-breaking): `Crypto > Fintech > Finance > Tech > Health > Sports > Services > Investors > Legal > Media > Merchants > Government > International > Associations > Advocacy-Political > Industrials`
- **Multiple matches**: When an organization matches multiple sectors, use precedence order
- **Regulators**: Always categorize by country, not by sector triggers
- **Confidence**: Return `high` when 3+ triggers match, `medium` for 1-2 matches, `low` for inference only
