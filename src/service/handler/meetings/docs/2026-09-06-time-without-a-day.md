# A time without a day means today

Requiring an explicit day made a request such as "Meet Jane at 2:30 PM"
unnecessarily stop at a blank date and a clarification question.

The AI now uses today when the request omits the day and states that
assumption in the editable draft. An explicit day always takes precedence.
The meeting keeps that date even if its time has already passed; creation
requires a future time, rather than silently moving the meeting to tomorrow.
Missing times still need to be supplied.
