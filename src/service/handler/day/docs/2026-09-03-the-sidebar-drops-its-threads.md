# The sidebar drops its Threads list

Under the days, the sidebar listed every live chat thread the service
held, from any day, for as long as it held it. Beside the day's own rail
that was a second, worse list of the same chats, and it was in the way.

The list is gone. The sidebar is New chat, Talk, the days, and the way out
to Automations, Explorer and Settings. A day's chats, filed and live, show
in its rail's Chats section, and a live one opens from there. The thread
page is unchanged, and so is the `/chat` poll behind it: the rail and the
thread page's title still read from it.
