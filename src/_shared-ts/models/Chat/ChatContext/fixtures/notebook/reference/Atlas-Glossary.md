# Atlas Glossary

Working definitions used across the Atlas project docs.

- **Rollout wave**: one region's worth of workspaces enabled together.
- **Canary window**: the first two hours after a wave, watched for error-rate regressions.
- **Freeze**: a period during which no schema migrations may run.
- **Tenant**: one customer workspace and all records under it.
- **Shard**: a storage partition holding a contiguous range of tenants.
