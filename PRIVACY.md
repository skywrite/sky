# Privacy

Sky is software you run on your own computer. It has no server of its own.

When you connect a Google account, Sky sets up a private Google Cloud
project inside that account and signs in to Google as an app that belongs
to you. The mail, calendar, documents and files it reads go into your own
notebook on your own disk. Nothing is sent to the people who make Sky.

Sky keeps your Google tokens in your computer's keychain. Removing the
account in Sky's settings deletes them; revoking the grant at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions cuts Sky off entirely.

Sky sends the text you ask it to work on to the AI providers you configure,
under your own API keys. Which providers, and what they see, is your choice.
